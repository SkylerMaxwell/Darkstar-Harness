# Contributing to Darkstar Harness

Darkstar Harness welcomes contributions while preserving both the public GPL release and the ability to offer separate proprietary commercial licenses.

## No-signature contributor agreement

First-party Darkstar Harness contributions are subject to [`CLA.md`](CLA.md).

**By intentionally submitting a contribution through an Official Contribution Channel that displays or conspicuously references the CLA before submission, you accept the CLA by submitting. You do not need to sign, comment, paste an acceptance statement, or take any other separate action.**

The standard Official Contribution Channel is the project's pull-request flow when the Darkstar Harness CLA notice is presented. Material sent through an issue, email, direct message, pasted snippet, or another channel that did not present equivalent notice is not automatically treated as a CLA-covered first-party contribution.

You keep ownership of your contribution. The CLA gives the **Darkstar Harness Licensor** the copyright, patent, sublicensing, and relicensing rights needed to distribute the contribution publicly under GPLv3 and, where the Licensor owns or controls sufficient rights in the resulting First-Party Code, to license that code separately under proprietary/commercial terms.

If an employer, client, university, or other entity owns or may own rights in your contribution, do not submit it unless that rights-holder has authorized the contribution and the CLA grants.

Clearly identify the source and license of any third-party material. Do not submit confidential, trade-secret, or incompatible third-party material.

## Before submitting

Run `npm --prefix backend/shell run verify`. Pull requests are checked with the same gate on Windows and Linux.
