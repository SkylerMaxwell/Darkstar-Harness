# Contributing to Darkstar Harness

Darkstar Harness is released under the Apache License 2.0. Contributions accepted into the project are distributed under the same Apache-2.0 license.

By intentionally submitting a contribution for inclusion in Darkstar Harness, you represent that you have the right to submit it and agree that the contribution may be distributed under Apache-2.0. You retain ownership of your contribution.

If an employer, client, university, or another entity owns or may own rights in your contribution, do not submit it unless that rights-holder has authorized the contribution. Clearly identify the source and license of any third-party material, and do not submit confidential, trade-secret, or license-incompatible material.

## Before submitting

Run `npm --prefix backend/shell run verify`. Pull requests are checked with the same gate on Windows and Linux.
